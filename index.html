THE EXPERIMENT

Good chemistry.
A little pressure.

  1. Pick your topics and add 2–8 players.
  2. Play on a shared device or join an online room. Answer the category on your turn.
  3. A correct, unused answer passes the flask. The round timer keeps running!
  4. Whoever holds it when time runs out loses one life. Last player with lives wins.

Practice mode has no timer or eliminations. Answers use a curated starter bank, not AI grading. Review the bank after each round; ask your TA to discuss any valid answer missing from it.
